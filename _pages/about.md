# About Me

I am a Postdoctoral Associate in the Department of Biostatistics at the Yale School of Public Health, working with Professor [Yize Zhao](https://ysph.yale.edu/profile/yize-zhao/). My research develops statistically grounded, uncertainty-aware methods that make modern AI, especially large language models (LLMs), efficient and trustworthy in high-stakes domains such as biomedicine, health, and education. At Yale, I am extending this work to electronic health records (EHR) data.

I received my Ph.D. in Statistics from the University of Georgia in 2026, where I worked in the Big Data Analytics Lab under the supervision of Professor [Ping Ma](https://bdalpingio.github.io/) and Professor [Wenxuan Zhong](https://zhonglabuga.github.io/). Before that, I received my M.S. in Statistics from the University of Wisconsin–Madison (2021) and my B.S. in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from Nankai University (2020).


# Research

My work is organized around three connected themes.

**Efficient and trustworthy AI.** Knowledge distillation compresses large models into compact ones, but standard methods match only point predictions and leave the smaller model poorly calibrated. I develop statistically principled distillation methods with built-in uncertainty quantification, from a Bayesian reformulation of distillation to fusing multiple teachers and using small models to help train large ones.
Representative work: [Bayesian Knowledge Distillation](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/fang24a.html) (ICML 2024), [Knowledge Cascade](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v27/23-0359.html) (JMLR 2026), [A Statistical Perspective on Knowledge Distillation](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-statistics-043025-102547) (Annual Review of Statistics and Its Application).

**Reliable AI for educational assessment.** I build automated scoring systems that are data- and compute-efficient, stay calibrated, and defer uncertain cases to human raters.
Representative work: [a knowledge-distilled multi-task mixture-of-experts scorer](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v40i48.42115) (AAAI 2026), [LLM-based data augmentation for imbalanced scoring data](https://doi.org/10.1111/jedm.70020) (Journal of Educational Measurement).

**Statistical machine learning for bioinformatics.** I develop methods that turn high-dimensional, noisy biological data into interpretable structure, including gene panel design and denoising for spatial transcriptomics and microbiome–metabolome association analysis.
Representative work: [optimal gene panel selection](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkag621) (Nucleic Acids Research), [SpaDiff](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.crmeth.2026.101530) (Cell Reports Methods), [MultiCOP](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12561-024-09450-9) (Statistics in Biosciences).

<h2 class="home-h2">About Me</h2>

<p class="home-lead">I am a Postdoctoral Associate in the Department of Biostatistics at the Yale School of Public Health, working with Professor <a href="https://ysph.yale.edu/profile/yize-zhao/">Yize Zhao</a>. I develop statistically grounded, uncertainty-aware methods that make modern AI, especially large language models (LLMs), efficient and trustworthy in high-stakes domains such as biomedicine, health, and education. At Yale, I am extending this work to electronic health records (EHR) data.</p>
